Strengths

  • No macro forecasting needed — price does the talking
  • Fully systematic, removes emotion from sector calls
  • Works across market cycles, not just bull runs

Limitations

  • Lags at turning points — momentum confirms after the fact
  • Broad sector ETFs blend strong sub-industries with weak ones
  • Whipsaws in choppy, range-bound markets

How it works

01

Measure returns

Calculate each sector's total return over the chosen lookback period vs the benchmark.

02

Rank all sectors

Sort every sector from highest to lowest return. No subjective judgement needed.

03

Buy the leaders

Allocate equal or momentum-scaled weight to the top-ranked sectors only.

04

Rebalance monthly

At month-end, re-rank and swap out any sector that drops out of the top tier.

05

Cut the laggards

Bottom-ranked sectors get zero allocation — momentum says avoid, regardless of valuation.
