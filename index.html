Method. Return = adjusted close at signal month-end ÷ adjusted close k months earlier − 1. Sectors are ranked high to low; the top N are bought equal-weight and held for the following calendar month, the bottom N are avoided (or sold if held). The middle sectors are a hold, not a new buy. The backtest ignores trading costs, taxes and slippage, and uses only months that are fully complete.
